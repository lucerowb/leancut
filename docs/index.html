How it works

Four passes, all on your machine

Each clip goes through the same pipeline. You review the result on a timeline before anything is rendered.

  1. Listen

    Pulls the audio out of every clip and drops silence, wind roar and engine drone with voice-activity detection.

    FFmpeg + Silero VAD

  2. Transcribe

    Turns the talking into timecoded sentences. Works in 99 languages and can translate to English as it goes.

    faster-whisper

  3. Judge

    A local language model scores every line from 1 to 5. Retakes, loud reactions and your spoken “clip that” markers count too. LeanCut keeps the best until your target length is reached.

    Ollama · LM Studio

  4. Cut

    Stitches the kept lines and the best silent scenery into one loudness-matched video, plus files to finish in your usual editor.

    MP4 · SRT · EDL · FCPXML · OTIO
Built for riders

Everything a long ride needs

Talking-head editors throw away everything without words. LeanCut knows a ride is more than talk.

  • RidesScenic B-roll keeperSilent stretches are scored for movement, colour and exposure; the best views stay in, at a share you choose.
  • RidesSay “clip that”Shout a marker phrase mid-ride and the 30 seconds before it are kept. The command itself is cut.
  • RidesLoud-moment boostShouts, laughs and revs that jump above the road noise lift the score of what was said there.
  • RidesRide-through fast-forwardLong dropped stretches can become a few seconds of time-lapse instead of a jump cut.
  • EditRetakes handledWhen you say a line twice, the earlier take goes. Whisper's repeat loops are cleaned up too.
  • EditDrag to trimEvery kept block has handles on the timeline. Tick, untick and preview any line.
  • PublishChapters, title & tagsA YouTube-ready description with valid chapters, written from what actually happens.
  • PublishSubtitles & ShortsAn .srt timed to the cut, and vertical 9:16 Shorts from the best continuous moments.
  • PublishThumbnail picksThe sharpest, best-exposed frames from the top-scored moments, saved as JPEGs.
  • SoundYouTube loudnessTwo-pass normalisation to −14 LUFS, optional wind and road-noise reduction, and click-free cuts.
  • SoundMusic that ducksPick a track and it loops under the video, dipping whenever someone speaks.
  • SpeedHardware encoding, resumableVideoToolbox or NVENC with GPU decoding. Cancel any time; a re-export reuses finished parts (28 s instead of 9 min in our 4K test).
  • WorkflowProjects & overnight batchKeep every trip as its own project and process a whole week of rides while you sleep.
  • WorkflowSecond-camera syncLine up a chest cam or phone with your helmet cam by their audio; the angle rides along into your editor.
  • WorkflowUpdates without trackingOne anonymous check against GitHub tells you when a new version is out. You can turn it off.

Why local

Your rides stay yours

  • No uploadsA 4K ride is tens of gigabytes. LeanCut reads it where it sits on your drive.
  • No subscriptionWhisper and the language model are free, open models running on your own CPU or GPU.
  • Works offlineAfter the one-time model download you can edit on a laptop at a campsite.
  • You have the final sayEvery line shows its score and reason. Tick or untick anything before you export.

What you need

Pick a model that fits your memory

The language model has to fit in RAM (on Apple Silicon, unified memory). LeanCut refuses models that would swap to disk, because those stall for hours.

Your memoryLanguage modelWhisper modelNotes
8 GBqwen2.5:3b or llama3.2:3bsmallWorks, rougher judgement
16 GBqwen2.5:7b or llama3.1:8blarge-v3-turboGood default

Runs on Apple Silicon Macs and 64-bit Windows 10/11. On Windows, transcription uses an NVIDIA GPU when CUDA 12 and cuDNN 9 are installed, and the CPU otherwise.
