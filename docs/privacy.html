Privacy policy

In one sentence: LeanCut never sends your footage, audio, transcripts or exports anywhere; they stay on your computer. The few network requests it does make are listed below.

LeanCut is open-source software published by its contributors ("we", "us"). You can check every claim on this page in the source code.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run any servers, and the app has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising. We don't receive your footage, transcripts, file names, device identifiers, IP address or usage information, and we have no way to.

Data stored on your computer

To work, LeanCut stores the following locally, in its data folder. None of it leaves your computer.

  • project.json: your clip list (file names and paths), the transcripts with their scores and reasons, which lines are kept, your edit (looks, titles, voice-over text), your conversation with the assistant and its suggestions.
  • settings.json: your settings.
  • taste.json: the text of up to 200 lines you kept or cut yourself, used as examples when scoring future edits.
  • Voice samples (voices/): if you use Use my voice from this project, a short WAV of your own speech taken from your footage. A sample you pick yourself is used where it is.
  • Cache (cache/): audio extracted from your clips, shot measurements, footage-search indexes, rendered parts and previews, and synthesised voice-over lines.
  • Models (models/): the optional vision and voice-enhancement models, once downloaded.
  • Copied clips (media/): clips you dragged onto the app. Clips you pick with the file picker are used where they are and never copied.
  • Exports (output/): the rendered video and edit lists.
  • leancut.log: a log of what the desktop app did, which can include clip names, folder paths and error messages.

To remove everything, quit LeanCut and delete its data folder. Removing a clip inside the app only deletes copies LeanCut made itself, never your original footage. Whisper models stay in ~/.cache/huggingface until you delete them, since other apps can share them.

Network activity

LeanCut makes only these network requests:

  • Model downloads (once each): the first time you use a Whisper model (or click Download in Settings › Models on disk), it's downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Optional features download their own model the first time you use them: face and licence-plate detectors and the CLIP search model from Hugging Face, and the voice-enhancement model (DeepFilterNet3) from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). If you installed voice cloning from source, the Chatterbox library downloads its model from Hugging Face too (see third parties). After that LeanCut uses the copies on your disk without contacting anyone. These requests ask for model files only; nothing about your footage is sent. The host sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • Your language-model server: to score a transcript, answer the assistant and write suggestions, LeanCut sends transcript text, a summary of your edit and your messages to the server you set as Base URL. If you choose a vision model for the visual judge, it also sends a few small still frames from your B-roll candidates to that server. By default that is Ollama (http://localhost:11434) or LM Studio (http://localhost:1234) on your own computer, so nothing leaves it. See below if you change it.
  • Your voice server (optional): if you choose the voice-server engine, the voice-over text and the path of your voice sample are sent to the URL you enter in Settings › Voice. voice_server.py runs on your own computer and only accepts local requests.
  • Update check (optional): when LeanCut starts, and when you click Check now, it sends one anonymous request to https://api.github.com/repos/lucerowb/leancut/releases/latest to see whether a newer version exists. Nothing about you, your computer or your footage is in it. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request (GitHub's privacy statement). Turn it off in Settings › Updates.
  • Update download (only when you click Install): the new version is downloaded from github.com/lucerowb/leancut/releases, which redirects to GitHub's file hosts (objects.githubusercontent.com or release-assets.githubusercontent.com). LeanCut refuses any other host.

The app's own interface talks to its backend over 127.0.0.1, which never leaves your computer, and the backend refuses requests from any other website. Once the models you use are downloaded, LeanCut works fully offline.

Your language-model server

LeanCut doesn't ship a language model or contact any AI provider by itself. It talks to whichever server you configure. If you point the base URL at a server on another machine or at a cloud provider, your transcript text, your assistant messages and, with a visual judge, a few still frames are sent there and are handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy, not this one. Your video and audio files are never sent. The same applies to a voice server on another machine: it receives your voice-over text.

Third parties

  • Hugging Face hosts the Whisper, detector, CLIP and Chatterbox models (Hugging Face privacy policy).
  • GitHub answers the optional update check, hosts releases, update downloads, the voice-enhancement model and this website.
  • Ollama, LM Studio or any other server you choose runs the language model under its own terms.
  • FFmpeg, Silero VAD, faster-whisper and ONNX Runtime run entirely on your computer and contact no one. Chatterbox (voice cloning, only if you installed it) runs on your computer too, but it's a third-party library that fetches its model from Hugging Face itself and may check there for a newer model when it loads.

We don't sell, share or transfer any data, because we don't receive any.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so Google receives your IP address when you visit, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Google privacy policy. The site itself uses no cookies or analytics.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app has no accounts and no content directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling ever changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before releasing that change. The full history is in the repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. Report security concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the repository's Security tab › Report a vulnerability).
